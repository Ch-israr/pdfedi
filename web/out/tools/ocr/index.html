OCR to Searchable PDF — what it does

Make scanned PDFs searchable and selectable. Optical character recognition reads the text in your scanned pages and adds an invisible text layer — the pages look identical, but you can now search, select, and copy the text. Pages that already contain text are skipped automatically.

How to use OCR to Searchable PDF

  1. 1Upload your scanned PDF (up to 50MB).
  2. 2Choose the document language (e.g. eng, eng+deu).
  3. 3Click Run OCR and wait — scanned pages are processed one by one.
  4. 4Download your searchable PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What does OCR do to a PDF?

OCR (optical character recognition) analyzes the images in a scanned PDF, recognizes the text, and embeds an invisible text layer. The pages look the same, but the text becomes searchable and selectable.

How long does OCR take?

It depends on page count and content — scanned pages are processed individually. Pages that already have text are skipped instantly, so mixed documents finish much faster.

Which languages are supported?

Set the language option with a Tesseract language code such as eng for English or eng+deu for English and German combined.

Will OCR change how my pages look?

No. The original scanned images are kept as the visible pages; only an invisible text layer is added underneath.

My PDF already has some text. Will it be re-OCRed?

No — pages with a usable text layer are detected and copied through unchanged. Only image-only pages go through recognition.
