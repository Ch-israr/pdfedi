Reorder Pages — what it does

Put your PDF pages in the right order. Specify the new page sequence and get a PDF with pages rearranged exactly as you want — no need to re-scan or rebuild the document.

How to use Reorder Pages

  1. 1Upload your PDF file (up to 50MB).
  2. 2Enter the new page order as comma-separated page numbers (e.g. 3,1,2,4).
  3. 3Click Reorder and download the rearranged PDF.

Key features

  • ✓Rearrange pages into any sequence
  • ✓Simple comma-separated page order input
  • ✓Content and quality fully preserved

When to use Reorder Pages

  • Fix pages scanned in the wrong order
  • Move the table of contents or cover to the front
  • Reorganize a report's sections
  • Correct page sequence before printing

Frequently asked questions

How do I rearrange pages in a PDF?

Upload your PDF and enter the desired page order as numbers separated by commas — for example 3,1,2,4 puts old page 3 first. Click Reorder to download.

Do I need to list every page?

Enter the full sequence of pages you want in the output. Pages not listed are not included, so list every page you want to keep.

Will reordering change my PDF's quality?

No. Pages are rearranged without touching their content, so quality is identical.
