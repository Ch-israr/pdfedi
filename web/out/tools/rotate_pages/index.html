Rotate Pages — what it does

Fix sideways or upside-down PDF pages. Rotate all pages or just selected page ranges by 90, 180, or 270 degrees — ideal for scanned documents that came out with the wrong orientation.

How to use Rotate Pages

  1. 1Upload your PDF file (up to 50MB).
  2. 2Choose the rotation angle: 90°, 180°, or 270°.
  3. 3Optionally limit rotation to specific pages (e.g. 1-3,5). Leave empty for all pages.
  4. 4Click Rotate and download the corrected PDF.

Key features

  • ✓Rotate 90°, 180°, or 270°
  • ✓Apply to all pages or selected page ranges
  • ✓Fixes sideways scanned documents
  • ✓No quality loss

When to use Rotate Pages

  • Correct pages scanned in landscape instead of portrait
  • Fix upside-down pages in a scanned batch
  • Rotate a single sideways page without affecting the rest
  • Standardize orientation before merging documents

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate a PDF page?

Upload your PDF, pick 90°, 180°, or 270°, optionally enter page ranges like 2-4, then click Rotate. Leave the pages field empty to rotate every page.

Can I rotate only some pages of a PDF?

Yes. Enter 1-based page ranges such as 1-3,5 and only those pages are rotated. All other pages stay as they are.

Will rotating reduce PDF quality?

No. Rotation changes the page orientation metadata and content positioning — your text and images keep their original quality.
