Page Thumbnails — what it does

See every page of your PDF at a glance. This tool renders a contact sheet — a single PNG image with small thumbnails of your pages arranged in a grid. Choose the number of columns, thumbnail width, and which pages to include.

How to use Page Thumbnails

  1. 1Upload your PDF file (up to 50MB).
  2. 2Set the number of columns, thumbnail width, and page ranges (optional).
  3. 3Click Generate and download the PNG contact sheet.

Key features

  • ✓Contact sheet of all or selected pages
  • ✓Adjustable columns and thumbnail width
  • ✓Single PNG output
  • ✓Up to 50 thumbnails per sheet

When to use Page Thumbnails

  • Preview a long document's layout at a glance
  • Check scan quality across all pages
  • Create a visual index of a PDF
  • Spot blank or damaged pages quickly

Frequently asked questions

What is a PDF thumbnail contact sheet?

A single image showing small previews of many PDF pages arranged in a grid — like a photographer's contact sheet. It lets you scan a whole document visually in seconds.

How many thumbnails can I generate?

Up to 50 thumbnails per contact sheet.

Can I choose which pages appear?

Yes — enter page ranges like 1-10, or leave empty to include all pages.
